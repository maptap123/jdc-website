Planning notes for lake lots

Lake communities usually have an owners’ association with its own design review in addition to county permits. Before drawings are final, we help you find out what the association requires, so approvals do not slow the schedule.

Dearborn County’s building department issues building permits across much of the county, while your city or town may handle zoning and location approvals separately. We confirm the exact path for your address before drawings are final, so there are no surprises at the permit counter.

Lake-facing walls get a lot of sun, wind and weather. We pay particular attention to window and door performance, deck framing and finishes, and drainage, since those are the parts of a lake house that wear first.

Renovate or build new?

If you own a lake house with a good view and a poor layout, a renovation can deliver most of what a new build would at a lower cost. If the structure itself is the limit, building new may make more sense. We will give you an honest comparison for your house, including rough costs for each path.
