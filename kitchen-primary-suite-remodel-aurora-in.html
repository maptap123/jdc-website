Kitchen, Primary Suite and Laundry Remodel

A traditional, farmhouse-inspired remodel in Aurora, Indiana covering the kitchen, primary suite, laundry and hall half bath.

26 photos

Remodeling the rooms a family uses most

This Aurora remodel focused on the rooms that matter most day to day: the kitchen, the primary suite, the laundry and a powder room, along with some exterior work. The goal was a traditional, farmhouse-inspired look with finishes that feel warm and a layout that works for how the owners actually live, including a few accessibility-related features built in for the long run.

The project ran roughly five to eight months, which included waiting on some delayed items. Here is a look at the finished rooms.

A farmhouse kitchen with a walnut island

The kitchen is built around a long island with a walnut butcher-block top, a warm contrast against the white painted cabinets and the white subway tile on the walls. Rustic metal-legged stools line one side, and a pair of turned corbels under the overhang give the island a furniture-like look.

A wide window over the farmhouse sink looks out across open fields, so washing up comes with a view. Floating dark wood shelves flank the window and the range, holding everyday dishes and cookbooks within reach, and a black chandelier with fabric shades hangs over the work area.

Details that make it feel custom

The details are where a remodel like this earns its character. Behind the range, a square panel of decorative tile breaks up the white subway tile and creates a focal point above the stove. Under the farmhouse sink, a fabric skirt replaces cabinet doors in the traditional style. And the island's turned corbels, set against the stools, add a small piece of craftsmanship you notice every time you sit down.

A dining nook with a dark accent wall

Off the kitchen, a small dining nook is painted in a deep charcoal with a set of decorative wall tiles, which sets it apart from the light kitchen and makes the space feel cozy. A little table with two upholstered chairs sits beside a window with plantation shutters.

A powder room with a pattern

The powder room is small, so it can afford to be bold. A black-and-white patterned tile floor sits below white wainscoting, with a small white vanity and a dark upper wall for contrast.

A primary bath with a clawfoot tub

The primary bath is a mix of classic and practical. A clawfoot tub sits beneath a window against a navy accent wall, on a herringbone-pattern floor. The vanity is white, with vessel sinks, a quartz-look top and large round black-framed mirrors, and the faucets have a vintage look that matches the rest of the house.

A tiled walk-in shower is designed around accessibility. It is finished in white subway tile with a black mosaic floor, grab bars on the walls and a handheld shower head on a slide bar, which makes it comfortable and safe for today and for years to come.

A remodel that works for the long run

Timelines on projects like this often stretch when special-order items arrive late, and that was true here. The end result is a home with warm finishes, a kitchen made for gathering and a primary suite designed to keep working as needs change.
