Contemporary Lake House Renovation

A contemporary lake house renovation in Hidden Valley Lake, Indiana, with a sunken great room, a large opening glass wall and a lake-facing deck.

38 photos

This Hidden Valley Lake renovation turned an older house into a contemporary lake home. The centerpiece is a sunken great room with a large opening glass wall that pulls the lake view into the living space.

Wood detailing warms the modern finishes, and an expansive lake-facing deck extends the living area outdoors. The scope also covered the primary suite, dining area, foyer, laundry, powder room and some upstairs work.

The renovation took about five months, finishing with final walkthrough and details in early fall.
