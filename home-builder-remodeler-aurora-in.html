What to know before you remodel or build in Aurora

Downtown Aurora is a designated historic district, and exterior changes to buildings there can require review by the city’s Historic Preservation Commission before a permit is issued. If your home is in or near the district, we check this first.

Dearborn County’s building department issues building permits across much of the county, while your city or town may handle zoning and location approvals separately. We confirm the exact path for your address before drawings are final, so there are no surprises at the permit counter.

Properties close to the Ohio River can fall inside a mapped floodplain, which can affect foundation, elevation and insurance. We look at flood maps early, so you know what applies before design work starts.

Working in older homes

Older homes tend to hide things: out-of-square walls, outdated wiring, plaster, and layouts that made sense a century ago. We walk through the house with you first and flag what we are likely to find, so the budget includes it from the start.
