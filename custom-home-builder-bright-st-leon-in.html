Building in north Dearborn County

Bright, St. Leon and West Harrison sit in the northern part of Dearborn County, near the Ohio line. The area suits families who want room and a home in Indiana while staying close to Harrison, I-74 and the west side of Cincinnati. Lots range from neighborhood parcels to several acres.

What to know before you build

Bright is unincorporated, so county rules apply there. Dearborn County's building department issues building permits across much of the county, and the county's planning and zoning department handles zoning. Towns such as St. Leon and West Harrison may handle zoning and location approvals themselves. We confirm the exact path for your address before drawings are final.

Not every lot is on public sewer. Where it is not, a septic system needs a permit from the county health department, which starts with a soil evaluation by a state-recognized soil scientist. Check this before you buy a lot, because it affects both the house plan and the budget.

Much of this area is rolling ground. Designing the house around the grade, often with a walkout lower level, usually produces a better home and a smaller site-work bill.
