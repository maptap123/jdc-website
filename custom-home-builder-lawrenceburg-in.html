What to know before you build or remodel in Lawrenceburg

Dearborn County’s building department issues building permits across much of the county, while your city or town may handle zoning and location approvals separately. We confirm the exact path for your address before drawings are final, so there are no surprises at the permit counter.

Parts of Lawrenceburg lie along the Ohio River, where flood maps can affect foundation and elevation requirements. We check the maps for your address early.

For remodels, older homes may hide surprises behind the walls. We walk through the house with you first and flag what we are likely to find.
