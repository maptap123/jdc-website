Frequently asked questions

How much does a custom home cost in Southeast Indiana?

It depends on size, finishes and the lot. Our custom homes typically range from about $500,000 to $2 million. We give a firm number through our pre-construction budget process, once we know your plans and your site.

How long does it take to build a custom home?

Construction on a JDC custom home typically takes about 5 to 9 months. Design, selections and permitting happen before construction begins, and larger or more complex homes take longer. We give you a realistic schedule at the start.

Do I need to own land before I talk to you?

No. We are glad to talk through a lot before you buy it, and we are also happy to build on a site you already own. See our pages on finding land and building on your own lot.

Will I need a septic system?

Many rural lots are not on public sewer. A septic system needs a county health department permit and a soil evaluation by a state-recognized soil scientist. We recommend checking this before you buy land.

Who handles permits?

We do. We confirm the permit path for your address, including county building permits and any local zoning, flood or association approvals, before drawings are final.

Do you help with the design?

Yes. Every home starts with a conversation about how you live. Our design team turns that into detailed plans and 3D renderings, so you see and approve the home before construction begins.

When are selections made?

Before construction. You work through cabinets, countertops, tile, fixtures, flooring, trim and paint with a detailed budget tracker, and nothing goes to contract until the selections are made and the costs are confirmed.

Will I know the price before you start?

Yes. Our detailed pre-construction budget process is designed so that you have a firm number before work begins, with selections made and costs confirmed.

Who will I work with during construction?

You have direct access to a project manager throughout the build, with regular updates and a documented punch list at each phase milestone.

What warranty do you offer?

JDC homes are backed by a comprehensive labor warranty, and we do a warranty walkthrough one year after move-in. We are a phone call away after the last tool leaves the site.

Do you build in my area?

Most of our work is in Dearborn County, including Lawrenceburg, Greendale, Aurora, Hidden Valley Lake, Guilford and New Alsace. See our Areas We Serve page, or send us your address to check.

Do you also remodel?

Yes. We remodel kitchens, bathrooms, additions and whole homes, so a family can work with the same team for a remodel now and a new home later.
